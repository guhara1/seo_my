Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NEXT SEO24 collects, uses and protects your personal data, in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

Last updated 3 June 2026.

Who we are

NEXT SEO24 (NEXT SEO24 Sdn Bhd), based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, operates this website and provides SEO, backlink, web, content and marketing services. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to NEXT SEO24.

What we collect

  • Information you provide. When you use our enquiry form or contact us via WhatsApp/email, we may collect your name, email address, website URL and the details of your enquiry.
  • Usage data. Standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed and referring source, collected to keep the site secure and to understand how it is used.
  • Cookies. We use a small amount of local storage to remember your language preference. Any analytics cookies, if enabled, are used only in aggregate.

How we use your data

We use your information to respond to enquiries, deliver and improve our services, send relevant updates you have asked for, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Sharing and storage

We may share data with trusted service providers (for example hosting, communication and analytics tools) strictly to operate our business, and only to the extent necessary. We retain personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described or as required by law.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may request access to, or correction of, your personal data, and you may withdraw consent or ask us to stop processing your data for marketing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, reach us via the contact section or WhatsApp. We will respond within a reasonable time.
