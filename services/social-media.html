What we actually do

Social media in Asia is not one market — it is several overlapping ones. Instagram and Facebook dominate purchase research in Malaysia, TikTok is the primary discovery channel for younger consumers, and LINE remains essential in Thailand and Taiwan. We build a platform mix that reflects where your buyers spend their time:

  • Channel strategy. We audit your audience data before choosing platforms, so budget goes where your buyers already are rather than wherever is trending in trade press.
  • Content production. Copywriting, graphics and short-form video scripts adapted to each platform's native format — not the same post copy-pasted across five channels.
  • Paid campaigns. Meta and TikTok advertising built around business outcomes: leads, sales and brand-search lift, with conversion tracking that links spend to revenue.
  • Community management. Timely, on-brand replies that show real people behind the account, because silence on comments sends its own message.

Social and SEO: the honest connection

We are straightforward with clients: likes and follower counts are not a direct Google ranking factor. What social does build is brand demand. When someone sees your brand repeatedly on TikTok and then searches for it by name, that branded search is a quality signal Google notices. Social also amplifies good content, earning links and mentions that do influence rankings. Done well, the two channels reinforce each other.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms do you manage?

We actively manage Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LINE. For clients targeting Greater China we also advise on Xiaohongshu. We recommend a focused two-platform start for most businesses rather than spreading thin across every channel at once.

Does social media help my SEO?

Not directly through ranking algorithms, but meaningfully in practice. Strong social presence increases branded search volume, earns organic mentions and links, and keeps your brand visible during the consideration stage. Those effects compound into better SEO results over time.

Do you run paid ads too?

Yes. Organic content and paid campaigns are planned together so they reinforce each other. A well-seeded organic post often outperforms a cold ad; a paid boost can give a high-quality post the initial reach it needs to gain traction on its own.
