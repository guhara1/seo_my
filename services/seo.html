What we actually do

SEO is the disciplined alignment of three things Google rewards: a site it can crawl easily, pages that answer the searcher's intent, and signals that show real expertise and trust. Every engagement starts with your own data — Search Console, analytics and live rankings — not guesswork:

  • Technical SEO. Crawlability, indexing, site architecture, structured data and Core Web Vitals (including INP), so nothing blocks Google from understanding your pages.
  • On-page optimization. Search intent mapping, titles, headings and internal links that make each page the clearest answer to its target query.
  • Content built to E-E-A-T. Pages that show first-hand experience, expertise and trust — with clear authorship — instead of thin, generic copy.
  • Measurement. Transparent monthly reporting tied to traffic, rankings and leads, so you can see exactly what changed and why.

Built for Google's current guidelines

Google's Helpful Content signals are now part of the core ranking system and work at the site level — a pile of low-value pages can drag down your good ones too. So we treat quality as a whole-site concern, not a per-page checkbox. We also follow the "who, how and why" principle: every page has a clear author, a clear reason to exist, and value a visitor cannot get from a generic summary elsewhere.

Just as important is what we never do: no keyword stuffing, cloaking, link schemes, doorway pages or mass-produced filler. These shortcuts are exactly what spam updates target, and recovering from a penalty costs far more than doing it right.

Frequently asked questions

How long until I see results?

SEO compounds. Most clients see meaningful movement in three to six months, with the strongest gains later as authority builds. If you need faster visibility, we pair SEO with paid search while the organic base grows.

Can you guarantee the #1 spot?

No honest agency can — Google's ranking factors are not for sale. What we commit to is durable, guideline-safe growth that keeps compounding instead of collapsing at the next update.

Do you work in Korean and Japanese?

Yes. We run native-language SEO for Korea and Japan, including Naver alongside Google, so you reach Asian audiences where they actually search.
