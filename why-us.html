Who we are

NEXT SEO24 is a small, senior digital marketing team based in Kuala Lumpur. Small is deliberate — every client works directly with experienced strategists, not account managers passing briefs to junior staff. Our team has native-language expertise across Korea, Japan and Southeast Asia, so work in each market feels locally fluent rather than imported.

Kuala Lumpur sits at the centre of Southeast Asia with strong ties to Korea and Japan. That geography shapes our thinking: strategies built on how people in each market actually search and what earns their trust.

How we work

Our process starts with your own data. We audit Search Console, analytics and existing rankings, then build from there. Everything follows Google's published guidelines — Helpful Content, E-E-A-T and the quality principles that matter more than ever as AI-generated content floods the web.

  • Guideline-first strategy. Every recommendation traces back to a Google guideline or documented best practice. We do not experiment with grey-area tactics.
  • Clear authorship and editorial standards. All content has a genuine reason to exist and expertise behind it. We do not publish pages purely to occupy rankings.
  • Transparent monthly reporting. A plain-language report each month covers rankings, traffic and leads with clear attribution — so you can see what changed and why.
  • No black-hat shortcuts. No link schemes, cloaking, doorway pages or filler content. These tactics attract penalties that cost far more to fix than they ever returned.

Frequently asked questions

Are you fully white-hat?

Yes, without exception. Every technique traces back to Google's own guidelines. We have turned down clients who wanted shortcuts, because the long-term integrity of our work matters more than any single contract.

How do you report results?

Monthly reports cover keyword rankings, organic traffic, conversions and technical changes made that period. Language is kept plain and every number is verifiable directly in your analytics and Search Console.

Which markets do you cover?

Our core markets are South Korea, Japan and Southeast Asia — primarily Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia. For Korea we work across Google and Naver. For Japan we cover Google and Yahoo! Japan. For Southeast Asia we work in English, Malay and Chinese.

Talk to us about your goals

Whether you are entering a new market or strengthening existing rankings, we will give you an honest picture of where you stand and what is achievable — no pressure, no jargon.
